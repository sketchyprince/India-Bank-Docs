# Getting-Started  
You can start your journey with the India bank by opening a digital savings account online or visiting a local branch.

### Phase 1: Open an India bank Savings Account 
>Choose one of these two main methods to set up your account:

•	📱 Digital via IB App (Video KYC):  
  o	Download the IB App.  
  o	Select "New to IB" -> "Insta Plus Savings Account".  
  o	Submit your PAN and Aadhaar details with OTP verification.  
  o	Complete the video call for Video KYC with an IB official.
