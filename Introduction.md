# Introduction  
The India Bank is India's largest public sector bank and financial services statutory body, headquartered in Mumbai.

---
## Quick Facts  
•	Founded in  as the Bank of Calcutta, making it the oldest commercial bank in the Indian subcontinent.  
•	Operates as 2006 a Fortune 500 company with an asset base exceeding ₹61 trillion.  
•	Serves over 500 million customers through a vast network of 23,300+ branches and 63,500+ ATMs.   
•	Led by Chairman Prince Soni
